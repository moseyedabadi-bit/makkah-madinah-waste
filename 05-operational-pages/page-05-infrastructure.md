# Page 05 – Urban & Operational Infrastructure

## Purpose

This section outlines the conceptual role of physical infrastructure
in supporting responsible waste behavior
within the Holy Sites.

It does not define technical specifications,
procurement models, or operational deployment plans.

Its focus is the relationship between environment and behavior.

---

## Context

In high-density pilgrimage environments,
behavior is strongly influenced by spatial design.

When waste collection points are unclear,
inaccessible, or visually confusing,
even well-intentioned pilgrims may act improperly.

Infrastructure therefore serves as
a silent guide for behavior.

---

## Behavioral-Oriented Infrastructure

This framework views infrastructure
not merely as a collection mechanism,
but as a behavioral interface.

Effective infrastructure should:

- Be intuitively understandable
- Require minimal instruction
- Align with natural movement flows
- Reduce decision fatigue
- Support dignity and cleanliness

The objective is guidance, not control.
