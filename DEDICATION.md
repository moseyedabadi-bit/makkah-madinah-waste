# Dedication

This work is dedicated to God,
the Most Merciful.

It is offered not as an achievement,
but as an intention.

Not for ownership,
not for recognition,
not for reward.

If it serves His creation,
that service is from Him.

If it brings benefit,
that benefit belongs to Him.

And if it contains flaws,
they are mine alone.

May this work be accepted
as a sincere attempt
to reduce harm,
preserve dignity,
and honor the sanctity
of the Holy Sites.

God knows best.
