# makkah-madinah-waste
# Makkah & Madinah – Responsible Waste Management Framework

A conceptual and ethical framework for responsible and circular waste management
in the Holy Sites of Makkah and Madinah.

This repository presents a structured sustainability model designed for
large-scale pilgrimage environments, including Hajj and Umrah,
where spiritual dignity, public cleanliness, and collective responsibility
must coexist.

---

## Context

Makkah and Madinah host the largest recurring religious gatherings in the world.
Millions of pilgrims from different cultures, languages, and countries
temporarily form a unique spiritual mega-city.

Despite continuous operational cleaning efforts, waste generation remains
one of the most sensitive environmental, ethical, and behavioral challenges
within the Holy Sites.

This framework approaches waste not merely as an operational issue,
but as a matter of stewardship, ethics, behavior, and collective responsibility.
