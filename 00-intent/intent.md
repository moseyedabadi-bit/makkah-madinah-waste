# Intent & Declaration of Purpose

## Statement of Intent

This repository is published with the sole intention of knowledge sharing,
ethical reflection, and conceptual contribution toward sustainable and
responsible waste management within the Holy Sites of Makkah and Madinah.

The content presented herein represents a conceptual framework only.

It is not intended as an operational proposal, implementation request,
commercial offer, tender submission, or funding application.

---

## Nature of This Work

This work constitutes:

- A conceptual sustainability framework
- A behavioral and ethical governance model
- A systems-level architectural vision
- A long-term policy-oriented reflection

It does not constitute:

- An executable project plan
- An institutional directive
- A legal mandate
- A governmental position
- A binding recommendation

---

## Non-Representation Clause

The author does not represent any government, authority, ministry,
religious institution, or private organization.

All views expressed are personal and conceptual in nature.

---

## Intellectual Stewardship

This work is shared under the principle of stewardship, not ownership.

The author does not claim exclusive possession of ideas related to
cleanliness, responsibility, or sustainability — values that are universal
and deeply rooted in Islamic ethics.

However, the specific structure, system design, documentation flow,
and integrated framework presented in this repository constitute
original intellectual work.

---

## Use and Attribution

This repository may be referenced for academic, research, and
non-commercial purposes with proper attribution.

Any practical implementation, adaptation, or institutional application
requires formal engagement and acknowledgment of authorship.

Unauthorized commercial or operational use without attribution
is not permitted.

---

## Ethical Foundation

The intention of this work is aligned with the principles of:

- Amanah (Trust)
- Nazafah (Cleanliness)
- Mas’uliyyah (Responsibility)
- Collective dignity of pilgrims
- Preservation of the sanctity of the Holy Sites

---

## Final Declaration

The ultimate ownership of all goodness belongs to God alone.

The author presents this work in humility,
seeking benefit, clarity, and ethical improvement for the Ummah.

No claim of authority, enforcement, or entitlement is intended.
