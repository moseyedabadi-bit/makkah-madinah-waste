## Conceptual Flow Summary

The framework follows a layered progression:

1. **Ethics before systems**  
2. **Behavior before infrastructure**  
3. **Collective responsibility before individual accountability**  
4. **Guidance before enforcement**  
5. **Dignity before incentive**  
6. **Vision before execution**

This order is intentional
and reflects the ethical logic of the model.
